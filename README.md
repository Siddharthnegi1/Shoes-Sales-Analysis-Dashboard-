## Shoes Sales Analysis

Project Overview - 
This project analyses shoe sales performance across multiple brands, shoe categories, sales channels, countries, and monthly trends using Tableau. The objective of the dashboard is to help businesses monitor revenue performance, identify high-performing segments, and support data-driven business decisions.
________________________________________
Dashboard Summary - 
The dashboard provides insights into:
•	Total Revenue Generated
•	Total Units Sold
•	Brand-wise Revenue Performance
•	Shoe Type Revenue Distribution
•	Sales Channel Analysis
•	Country-wise Revenue Contribution
•	Monthly Revenue Trend
________________________________________
Key Metrics - 
Total Revenue :	$1,453,186
Total Units Sold :	10,584
Total Customers :	1,000
________________________________________
Business Insights - 
1. Brand Performance Analysis:
The brand-wise revenue analysis shows that:
1.	Skechers generated the highest revenue among all brands.
2.	New Balance and Adidas also contributed significantly to total sales.
3.	Nike generated comparatively lower revenue than the other brands shown in the dashboard.
Insight : 
Customers are showing stronger purchasing preference toward Skechers and New Balance products, indicating better market demand or pricing strategy effectiveness.
________________________________________
2. Shoe Type Revenue Split:
The revenue contribution by shoe type reveals:
•	Sneakers contributed the largest revenue share.
•	Boots and Formal shoes also performed strongly.
•	Sports shoes generated the lowest revenue share.
Insight : 
Fashion-oriented and lifestyle footwear categories are outperforming performance-focused sports footwear.
________________________________________
3. Sales Channel Performance:
Revenue across sales channels is relatively balanced:
•	Retail Stores generated the highest revenue.
•	Mall sales closely followed.
•	Online sales also contributed significantly.
Insight : 
Customers still prefer offline shopping experiences for footwear purchases, but online sales remain an important growth opportunity.
________________________________________
4. Country-wise Revenue Analysis:
Top-performing countries include:
•	UAE
•	USA
•	France
•	Germany
•	Saudi Arabia
Insight : 
International markets are contributing strongly to overall revenue generation, especially premium and urban consumer markets.
________________________________________
5. Monthly Revenue Trend:
The monthly trend indicates:
•	Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year.
•	Strong sales peaks appeared during July and October.
•	Revenue dipped during August and September.
Insight : 
Seasonal buying behaviour and promotional campaigns likely influenced peak revenue months.
________________________________________
Recommendations - 
1. Focus on High-Performing Brands:
•	Increase marketing investment for Skechers and New Balance.
•	Expand product lines for top-performing brands.
•	Use customer feedback analysis to understand why these brands outperform competitors.
2. Strengthen Sneaker Category:
•	Launch more sneaker collections and limited editions.
•	Introduce premium sneaker campaigns targeting younger audiences.
•	Increase inventory allocation for high-demand sneaker products.
3. Improve Online Sales Growth:
•	Enhance website and mobile shopping experience.
•	Run targeted digital marketing campaigns.
•	Offer online-exclusive discounts and loyalty programs.
4. Expand in High-Revenue Countries:
•	Focus regional marketing strategies on UAE, USA, and France.
•	Improve supply chain efficiency in top-performing countries.
•	Analyse customer preferences by region for localized product offerings.
5. Reduce Seasonal Revenue Drops:
•	Launch promotional campaigns during low-performing months.
•	Introduce festive or seasonal product bundles.
•	Use predictive analytics for better inventory planning.

Conclusion - 
The Shoes Sales Analysis dashboard successfully provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance across brands, categories, countries, and channels. The analysis highlights strong-performing brands and product categories while also identifying opportunities for growth in online sales and seasonal performance optimization.
The dashboard enables business stakeholders to make informed strategic decisions related to inventory planning, marketing investment, customer targeting, and regional expansion.
